# Varuna Motion Components Pvt Ltd: Assembly-line investigation
Fictional training case, synthetic data. Nothing here is proprietary.

There are **two separate exercises**:
1. `objective-investigate.md`: identify a consequential question the production team is not asking.
2. `objective-replay.md`: build a visual timeline of where work waits and why the constraint changes.

**Complete both unless your instructor assigns just one.** Read `data/README.md` before beginning. All evidence is in `data/`; you may use any AI agent or programming tools. Investigate, don't merely accept a pleasing narrative. Roles in `data/people/` are people you may ask the instructor to simulate.

**Submit `replay.html` (required):** one HTML file containing an actual playable/scrubbable, timestamped replay **and** your investigation, evidence, rejected hypotheses and recommendation. It must open offline in a browser directly from disk, without importing other files or using CDNs, APIs or network requests. Embed the data that the replay uses. Hosting a copy online is optional; always submit the standalone file. **One `replay.html` can cover both exercises.** `SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md` suggests a review-friendly HTML layout, but is not a required template.
